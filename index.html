About

WellMusic is a small lofi label run by one person and a pile of machines. Tracks are generated with AI, then listened to and picked by hand — only what actually sounds good makes it out.

Every video gets its own artwork: a still scene brought to life with slow camera breathing, drifting light and weather. New tracks land as Shorts, longer sets come out as mixes you can leave running.
