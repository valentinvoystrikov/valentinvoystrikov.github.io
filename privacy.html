Privacy Policy

WellMusic · Lofi Beats — last updated: August 4, 2026

WellMusic ("the app") is a personal, non-commercial tool operated by the owner of the WellMusic · Lofi Beats channels. It uploads the owner's own music videos from the owner's private server to the owner's own accounts on video platforms (TikTok, YouTube).

What data we collect

None. The app does not collect, store, sell, or share any personal data of visitors or third parties. It has no user accounts, no analytics, no cookies, and no tracking.

Platform authorization

To upload content, the app uses the official TikTok and YouTube APIs. Authorization tokens are granted by the channel owner via OAuth and are stored only on the owner's private server. These tokens grant upload access solely to the owner's own accounts and are never shared with third parties. Authorization can be revoked at any time in the account settings of the respective platform.

Content

All uploaded music is generated with AI tools and reviewed ("curated") by a human before publication. Uploaded videos are clearly labeled as AI-generated content where the platform provides such labeling.

Contact

Questions about this policy: contact the channel owner via TikTok @wellmusic.lofi.
